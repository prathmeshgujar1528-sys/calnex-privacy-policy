CalNex Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Welcome to CalNex. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CalNex mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

CalNex is primarily a calculator and utility application. We do not require you to create an account to use the core calculator features.

Calculator inputs and results may be stored locally on your device to provide features such as calculation history. This locally stored information is not intended to be transmitted to CalNex servers.

2. Calculation Data

Information entered into calculators, such as numbers, calculation values, and results, may be stored locally on your device for application functionality.

CalNex does not intentionally collect this calculation data on its own servers.

3. Advertising

CalNex may display advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

CalNex may use App Open Ads when the application is opened or brought to the foreground. Ad availability and frequency may vary depending on Google's advertising systems and policies.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policies and advertising documentation.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by CalNex may be used to:

  • Provide calculator and utility functionality.
  • Maintain calculation history on the device.
  • Improve application functionality and stability.
  • Display advertisements through third-party advertising services.

5. Data Storage

Some application information may be stored locally on your device. You can generally remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the app, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

6. Third-Party Services

CalNex may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. CalNex does not control the data practices of third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

CalNex is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not intentionally request personal information from users through the core calculator features.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You may stop using CalNex at any time. Locally stored application data can generally be removed through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Advertising and privacy choices provided by Google or your device may also be available depending on your location, device, and applicable regulations.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published at this same webpage.

You do not need a new Privacy Policy URL whenever the content is updated. The existing URL can continue to be used.
